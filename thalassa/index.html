Thalassa

by Millwater Games

An atmospheric strategy game of the ancient Aegean, played on a globe you can hold. Found cities, take to the sea, win the favour of the gods and leave a legend behind.

The game

  • A world you can hold. One continuous zoom takes you from a vista on the horizon to the whole globe and back down to any shore.
  • The sea is the stage. Coasts decide where armies can land, and the seasons change the sea: in winter the open ocean is stormy and fleets hug the coast.
  • Armies as formations. Hoplites lock shields, flanks matter, and a beaten army can rout instead of dying.
  • Gods, oracles and heroes. Choose a patron god, follow the Oracle's prophecies, hold the Panhellenic Games, and raise heroes whose deeds are written into an illustrated epic.
  • Many roads to glory. Win by Glory, by holding the sacred Omphaloi, or by conquest.

Alone or together

Play alone against rival powers led by the computer, after a first voyage that teaches you the game. Or sail together with two to four friends: everyone moves at once each round, the computer leads the other powers, and games are found by a five-letter code or in a list of voyages open to all. Playing together uses Sign in with Google and Epic Online Services.

Platforms

Thalassa is in development for Android phones and tablets.
